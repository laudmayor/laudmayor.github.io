New Zealand Premium

PAYE includes income tax and the ACC earners' levy (1.75%, capped on tax code M). Official employee KiwiSaver rates are 3.5%, 4%, 6%, 8% or 10%. Allowances and leave loading are treated as taxable gross.

Source: IRD PAYE rates and ACC earners' levy, 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. ird.govt.nz tax codes, ACC levy, student loan, KiwiSaver.

United States Premium

Uses the IRS Pub 15-T percentage method for a standard W-4 with no extra adjustments, plus Social Security 6.2% up to the 2026 wage base and Medicare 1.45%. Additional Medicare 0.9% applies when annualised wages exceed $200,000. Allowances and leave loading are treated as taxable gross.

Source: IRS Publication 15-T (2026) and Topic 751, for use in 2026. IRS.gov/Pub15T, Topic 751, SSA wage base.

Canada Premium

Uses the CRA T4127 simplified annualised method with the basic personal amount, plus CPP (and CPP2 above the YMPE) and EI. Provincial tax is a flat percentage you enter, not the official provincial tables. Allowances and leave loading are treated as taxable gross.

Source: CRA T4127 123rd edition, effective 1 July 2026 (federal rates from the 122nd edition, 1 January 2026). T4127 July 2026, CPP rates, EI rates.
